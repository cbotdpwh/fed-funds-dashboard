    How requests are grouped

    • Dredging by administration: fund types that pay to run the equipment with DPWH's own crew (Operational, Labor, OSH, Bunkhouse, Office Supplies).
    • Preventive, corrective, dry docking and repair, accessories and mobilization follow the Fund Type column.
    • OKB: the request's remarks mention Oplan Kontra Baha or OKB. OKB cuts across all request types.
